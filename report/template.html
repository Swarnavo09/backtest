Nifty Trend Spread

Sell the ATM option in the direction of the trend and buy a cheaper option further out as the hedge. A bullish signal opens a bull put spread; a bearish signal opens a bear call spread. Signals come from EMA, MACD and Supertrend on the Nifty chart. This page covers the rules, the risk plan at 1:2, real backtest results on 5, 10 and 15 minute candles (a one-year month-by-month test at 1 vs 4 lots first, then the original 59-day test), how to set it up in an algo builder, and the margin.

    Short answer

      Overview

      How the strategy works

      Every candle close runs the same checks. A trade opens only when all three indicators agree, and it always goes in with the hedge leg first. Each trade has a stop-loss and a target fixed at 1:2, a trailing rule, and a hard exit at 14:45, before the broker’s MIS auto square-off.

      Structure

      The two spreads

      Strikes are in steps of 50. ATM is the strike nearest to Nifty spot at entry. The hedge sits a fixed distance away from the sold strike, so the most you can lose is capped at (width − credit) × 65 per lot.

          Stage 2: with the structural fixes

          Three changes follow from the stage-1 diagnosis. First, sell 100 points ITM, ATM or 100 points OTM, to change how much the spread moves per Nifty point. Second, add an optional 1-hour Supertrend filter, so you only trade with the bigger trend. Third, limit trades to 1 or 2 a day.

          Capital

          Margin for one lot

          Nifty lot size is 65 from January 2026. Selling one ATM option alone (naked) blocks roughly 8–9% of the contract value. Buying the hedge first cuts this sharply, because the most the position can lose is capped.

          Practical capital per lot: keep about ₹75,000–₹1,00,000 in the account per lot. That covers margin, the hedge premium, a string of stop-losses and margin spikes on volatile days. Do not trade the sold leg on its own expiry day: SEBI adds an extra 2% exposure margin on short options expiring that day, and gamma risk is highest then. The backtest rolls to the next week’s expiry on expiry day.
