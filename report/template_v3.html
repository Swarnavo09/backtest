Nifty Trend Spread

This version only trades when the bigger candles agree. On those days it sells a hedged spread in that direction on a 15-minute signal, then holds it until 14:45 unless the stop is hit. All results are after brokerage, STT, exchange charges, SEBI fee, stamp duty, GST and slippage. Every option leg is priced from real NSE closing premiums.
