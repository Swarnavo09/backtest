How the trade plays out: risk ₹3,250 (50 pts). At +100 pts the stop moves to cost. After that, the stop trails 100 pts behind the best price. A trade that runs to +300 pts exits at the ₹19,500 target. Every winner closes at least at break-even; trades that reach the target return 6× the risk.
If the dropdown has no "Over All Loss" option, ask Algorooms support how to set a combined-MTM stop loss. Don't put a 50-pt SL on each leg separately: that isn't the same as a 50-pt stop on the combined position.
