Long signal → Buy CE + Sell PE (synthetic long). Short signal → Buy PE + Sell CE (synthetic short). Both legs must use the same ATM strike and the same expiry.
Trail SL 40 / 20 means: every time a leg moves 40 points in your favour, that leg's stop moves 20 points. A leg stop of 20 points on each leg is about 40 points of Nifty movement. Uncheck "Add Signal Candle Condition".
