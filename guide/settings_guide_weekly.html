Risk ₹8,000 per trade. At +₹16,000 the stop moves to +₹300. After that, the locked profit rises ₹1,600 for every ₹1,600 of new profit, so it stays about ₹16,000 behind the best. Exits at ₹40,000. Any of these M2M exits ends trading for that week, as intended; the EMA21 exit does not.
